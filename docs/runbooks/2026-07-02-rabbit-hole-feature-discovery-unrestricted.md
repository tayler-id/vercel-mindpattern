Companion to `2026-07-02-rabbit-hole-new-feature-discovery.md` (the website-centered
30, which stands untouched). This is **v2 of the unrestricted pass** — the same-day v1
was written from the codebase study plus standard playbook patterns without dedicated

landscape:

1. **Agentic commerce** — how agents pay (x402, Stripe MPP, Cloudflare Monetization
   Gateway, MCP monetization, pay-per-crawl economics)

   Good Judgment, ICE/Polymarket, Doomberg)

## 1. What the research actually says (and what it killed)

**The thesis, sharpened by evidence:** MindPattern's edge is not traffic — it is
*structured, provenance-linked, nightly-updating intelligence*. In 2026 that asset
monetizes through three channels that barely existed 18 months ago, plus classic
human products that the evidence says still work:

- **Machines are becoming paying customers.** Stripe launched the Machine Payments
  Protocol in March 2026 with Anthropic and OpenAI among 100+ adopters
  ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/machine-payments-protocol)); x402 moved under the
  Linux Foundation in April with Coinbase, Cloudflare, Google, Visa, AWS, and
  Anthropic as core members ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/x402/));
  Cloudflare's Monetization Gateway (announced 2026-07-01) lets a site charge for
  pages, datasets, and MCP tools with no payment plumbing
  ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/)). Sacra — a
  10-person research shop — already ships MCP access in every pricing tier
  ([sacra.com/pricing](https://sacra.com/pricing/)).
- **The citation economy opened to indies this week.** Cloudflare's Pay Per Use
  (launched 2026-07-01) pays when content *appears in an AI answer*, not when crawled
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/cloudflares-new-policy-pushes-ai-companies-to-pay-for-publishers-content/));
  Perplexity's Comet Plus pool ($42.5M, 80/20 split) pays on citations *and agent
  actions*, 2,400+ publishers enrolled, entry via publishers@perplexity.ai
  ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/how-perplexity-new-revenue-model-works-according-to-its-head-of-publisher-partnerships/));
  ~20% of TollBit's 7,000 publisher sites earn "hundreds of dollars to tens of
  thousands per month"
  ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/the-washington-posts-arc-xp-adds-tollbit-to-help-publishers-make-money-from-ai-bot-traffic/)).
- **Being the canonical citation is a business asset.** Layoffs.fyi (solo side
  project → cited by WSJ/NYT/Reuters), Epoch AI (public trackers → paid contracts
  with Google DeepMind, OpenAI, xAI, UK government —
  [epoch.ai/our-funding](https://epoch.ai/our-funding)), LMArena (leaderboard →
  $100M seed at $600M —
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/21/lm-arena-the-organization-behind-popular-ai-leaderboards-lands-100m/)).
  The GEO evidence: original statistics lift AI-citation odds ~32%, quotations ~41%,
  visible citations ~30%; lists get cited ~3x prose; AI referrals convert 4.4-23x
  organic ([GEO research line](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735),
  [Demand Local](https://www.demandlocal.com/blog/ai-referral-traffic-conversion-rate-statistics/)).
- **Humans still pay for artifacts, calibration, and ritual.** The Information sells
  structured *databases* as its $749/yr upsell; Doomberg's $1,200/yr tier (one
  monthly Zoom) drives ~20% of revenue; Good Judgment sells forecast monitoring at
  $20k/yr; HBR's archive-grounded "Ask AI" hit 25% subscriber adoption with repeat
  use ([DCN](https://digitalcontentnext.org/blog/2026/01/22/which-audience-facing-ai-initiatives-are-publishers-seeing-success-with/)).

**What the research killed from v1:**
- *Generic crawl-toll optimism* — per-crawl monetization pays small publishers
  "effectively zero" ([Leaky Paywall](https://leakypaywall.com/cloudflare-pay-per-crawl-income-or-spare-change/));
  and ~half of x402's headline volume was wash-traded
  ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/03/11/coinbase-backed-ai-payments-protocol-wants-to-fix-micropayment-but-demand-is-just-not-there-yet)).
  Sell per-query value and dossiers, not bytes.
- *AI video anchors* — Channel 1 never launched to consumers; audiences rejected
  synthetic faces ([Futurism](https://futurism.com/the-byte/ai-generated-news-anchors-channel-1)). Cut.
- *JSON-LD as a citation lever* — Ahrefs found no citation uplift from schema alone;
  the win is *visible* structure (tables, one-line answers, Q&A blocks) and schema's
  indirect effects on Google AIO + RAG accuracy
  ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/)).
- *Ads/job-board/gift-sub filler* — replaced by researched mechanisms with real
  comparables.

  Room (the one feature with 25%-adoption evidence), then #17 Founding, #18 Liquid
