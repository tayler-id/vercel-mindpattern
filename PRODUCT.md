# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MindPattern serves builders, researchers, founders, and technical operators who need a fast read on the AI ecosystem without opening a chat first. They arrive looking for current briefings, recurring patterns, source trails, and practical links into the original material.

## Product Purpose

MindPattern is an autonomous AI research intelligence archive. It turns daily agent runs into readable briefings, topic clusters, source rankings, and source-linked findings. Success means a reader can understand what changed, choose a topic path, open the relevant briefing, and follow external sources without needing to ask the system a question.

## Brand Personality

Precise, skeptical, operational. The interface should feel like a live research desk: dense but legible, source-forward, current, and direct.

## Anti-references

Do not make the homepage a chat prompt, generic SaaS landing page, rounded card wall, or marketing hero. Do not hide evidence behind vague labels. Do not use shadcn blocks as the visual model for the new homepage. The visual reference is the Stitch Studio MindPattern command-console system: charcoal surface, zinc rules, mono labels, emerald status, amber secondary accents, dense grouped lists, and rectangular controls.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the archive, not the assistant.
2. Group content by reader intent: daily briefings, agents, topics, patterns, and source trails.
3. Make every link carry information scent with a title, date, topic, and short summary.
4. Preserve provenance: surface outbound sources wherever the data includes `source_url`.
5. Keep the UI operational: compact, fast to scan, keyboard-readable, and honest about data freshness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Do not rely on color alone for source, topic, or status meaning. Keep motion subtle, non-essential, and compatible with reduced-motion preferences. Use descriptive link text for internal articles and outbound source links.
